
# Bellabeat Smart Device Usage Analysis

**Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate | Capstone Project**

## Project Overview
This case study examines smart-device activity and sleep data to identify consumer behavior patterns and develop potential marketing strategies for Bellabeat's Leaf wellness tracker.

## Business Objective
Identify trends in smart-device usage and determine how those insights could inform marketing recommendations for Bellabeat.

## Tools Used
- Google Sheets: Data cleaning, validation, analysis, pivot tables, and visualizations
- Google Docs: Case study documentation and presentation

## Dataset
Public Fitbit Fitness Tracker Data, available on Kaggle, covering April 12 to May 12, 2016.

- Daily activity: 940 records from 33 participant IDs
- Sleep: 410 records from 24 participant IDs after removing 3 duplicates

[Dataset source](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/fitbit)

## Key Findings
1. **Activity and calories:** Daily steps and estimated calories burned showed a moderate positive correlation (Pearson r = 0.592).
2. **Sleep duration:** Median recorded sleep duration was 432.5 minutes, approximately 7.2 hours.
3. **Data coverage:** Weekday step rankings changed when records were restricted to those with activity-minute totals of 1,440. Saturday shifted from the highest average to the lowest, showing that conclusions were sensitive to the filtering choice.

## Business Recommendations
- Test marketing messages emphasizing awareness of personal activity patterns.
- Test sleep-pattern messaging and measure its impact on Leaf purchase conversion.
- Collect additional user data before optimizing activity reminder timing, and compare personalized reminders against fixed schedules.

## Limitations
The data is historical (2016), includes a small sample, and does not establish the behavior or preferences of Bellabeat customers. Correlation does not establish causation, and findings should be validated before influencing major marketing decisions.
